# Mezora two-minute demo recording

Record actual product screens and verified receipts. English narration below targets two minutes; check timing in the recorded delivery. This is a recording script, not a finished video.

| Time | Screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Workbench overview | A business wallet tells you what you hold. It does not tell you how much you have already promised to spend. Mezora brings MUSD bills, committed cash and payment evidence into one workspace. |
| 0:15–0:35 | Bill and cash commitment | Start with a bill. Review its amount, recipient and terms, then reserve cash for it. The bill becomes an onchain commitment, so upcoming payments are visible before the money leaves the treasury. |
| 0:35–0:50 | Cash plan and invoice checks | A thirty-day cash plan shows upcoming commitments and possible shortfalls. Invoice checks flag duplicates and changed recipients. The operating buffer helps an owner decide what cash should stay available. |
| 0:50–1:15 | Completed V2 page and receipt | For shared approval, this separate V2 test treasury requires owner and reviewer signatures in the contract. We funded it with one test MUSD, collected separate approvals to reserve and pay, and verified the payment receipt. The displayed recipient received exactly one MUSD. |
| 1:15–1:35 | Customer invoice and received payment | Incoming payments can be checked against a confirmed MUSD transfer and allocated to a customer invoice by the owner. This example is a self-funded test, not customer revenue. Statements keep the supporting records available for review. |
| 1:35–1:50 | Repayment and two handoff receipts | Mezora also reads a Mezo borrower position and prepares a partial repayment. Withdrawing treasury cash and repaying debt are two separate approvals and transactions. The owner stays in control of both. |
| 1:50–2:00 | App and source links | Mezora is a working Mezo Testnet prototype. The main workspace uses V1, while shared V2 payment acceptance is demonstrated separately. Contracts are unaudited. Try the app and inspect the source and receipts. |

## Capture checklist

- Use one desktop viewport in the extension-enabled browser. Keep unlock screens, private documents and unrelated tabs out of frame.
- Show the completed V2 page and explorer receipt. Do not send another payment just to capture it.
- Label simulated scenario segments visibly. Do not present them as live transactions.
- Do not edit V1 and V2 together as a seamless migrated workflow.
- Export, check the final duration and play the full video before attaching its link.
